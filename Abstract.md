# ResumeAI Recruitment and Candidate Matching System

## Abstract

ResumeAI is an implemented web application for organizing early-stage recruitment and recruiter review. Candidates create accounts, browse vacancies, answer job-specific screening questions, provide a resume, and complete skill tests after shortlisting. Recruiters manage jobs and questions, inspect applications, and make hiring decisions. The system uses a Next.js frontend, a FastAPI REST backend, and SQLAlchemy-backed SQLite by default, with optional MySQL support. JWT authentication and resource-ownership checks separate candidate and recruiter access.

For jobs with screening answers, the application is saved before external checks begin. Free-text answers are assessed separately by an AI-writing detector and a web-source similarity service; multiple-choice answers are excluded. A likely-AI or inconclusive detector result, high source similarity, or an insufficient, unavailable, or unconfigured check sends the application to recruiter-only manual review. This is a review route, not proof of AI use or copying, and it does not automatically reject a candidate. Internal flags and provider details are hidden from candidate views. Once checks clear, a candidate without an existing resume can upload a PDF or DOCX. Resume information is parsed for skills, experience, education, projects, and certifications; direct contact details and profile links are redacted before external language-model processing.

When configured, Groq-based matching compares the parsed profile and, when available, screening answers with the job requirements. It produces a match score across work experience, projects, screening responses, skills, education, and certifications; the screening-response category is omitted when there are no screening answers. A separate match-analysis originality estimate below 40 also sends an application to manual review. Recruiters can search and filter applications, review the separate integrity signals, and shortlist or invite candidates in bulk. Applicant identity is pseudonymous by default; identity reveals and other sensitive review actions are audit-logged. ResumeAI supports recruiter judgment with structured evidence but does not make hiring decisions automatically.

**Keywords:** recruitment software, résumé parsing, applicant tracking, candidate matching, screening questions, AI-content detection, web-source similarity, recruiter review
